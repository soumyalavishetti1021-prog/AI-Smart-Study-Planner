AI Smart Study Planner 📚

About the Project
AI Smart Study Planner is a web application designed to help students organize their study schedules, manage academic tasks, and monitor their learning progress.

Key Features
- Personalized study schedule generation
- Study task and subject management
- Learning progress tracking
- Better time management and productivity
- User-friendly interface

Benefits
- Helps students plan study sessions around their available time.
- Makes it easier to prioritize subjects and pending tasks.
- Helps identify study progress and areas that need more attention.
- Encourages consistent study habits and reduces last-minute preparation.
